公司简介

About Us
中文版

河北盛誉光电科技有限公司旗下品牌「怡彩 YICAI」，专注于户外照明灯具的研发、生产与销售，产品涵盖道路照明、庭院照明、景观亮化、草坪点缀等多个系列，广泛应用于市政道路、住宅小区、公园景区与商业街区。

公司坚持以品质为本，光源、电器与结构件均选用可靠供应商，出厂前经过严格老化测试，让每一盏灯都经得起户外的日晒雨淋。

  • 工厂直供，价格实在
  • 支持按工程需求定制规格
  • 质保完善，售后响应及时
  • 承接市政、园区、小区等照明工程项目
English

YICAI is the brand of Hebei Shengyu Optoelectronic Technology Co., Ltd., specializing in the R&D, manufacturing and sales of outdoor lighting products, including street lights, garden lights, landscape lights and lawn lights. Our products are widely used on municipal roads, in residential communities, parks, scenic areas and commercial districts.

Quality comes first: light sources, electrical components and structural parts are all selected from reliable suppliers, and every lamp passes strict aging tests before delivery to withstand outdoor conditions.

  • Factory-direct supply at competitive prices
  • Custom specifications available for engineering projects
  • Reliable warranty with responsive after-sales service
  • Undertaking municipal, park and community lighting projects
