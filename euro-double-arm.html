English

A classic European retro double-arm garden light with cast aluminum pole, tapered base, scroll and shell ornaments, and symmetric double arms - stately and full of vintage charm.

Two pear-shaped lamp heads with transparent tempered glass shades offer excellent light transmission and weather resistance. 3000K warm white creates a cozy atmosphere; bat-wing light distribution ensures even road illumination without glare.

Optional cast iron flower pots on top and flagpole holes on the pole, easy to install and remove. Inspection port 0.5m above ground and replaceable light source reduce maintenance cost. Secondary design customization available.

常见问题

FAQ
双臂可以分开控制吗？

可以。双臂独立控制是标配，可搭配时控或智能控制器实现上半夜双亮、深夜单亮，节能明显。

铸铝灯杆和钢灯杆哪个好？

铸铝不易生锈、造型还原度高，适合景观要求高的项目；钢杆承重与抗风更强，适合多风地区，可按项目选配。

平均照度能到多少？

蝙蝠翼配光，15米间距下路面平均照度≥15lx，均匀度满足步道照明标准。

质保多久？

灯体与灯杆质保3年，光源与驱动质保2年。
