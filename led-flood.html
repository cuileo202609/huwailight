户外LED投光灯（洗墙/照树款）

Outdoor LED Flood Light
中文版

户外防水LED投光灯，含洗墙、照树、地埋、插地、抱树、立杆、底座等多种安装款式，广泛应用于广场、步道、草坪、树池、景墙等户外亮化场景。

高亮度LED芯片（COB/SMD可选），功率10W-100W可定制，色温3000K-6000K及RGB全彩可选，发光角度15°/24°/36°/60°灵活选择。压铸铝灯体+钢化玻璃面罩，静电喷塑处理，IP65-IP67防护，工作温度-30℃~+50℃，LED寿命≥50000小时。

支持常亮、0-10V调光、DALI、蓝牙Mesh、DMX512等多种控制方式，满足从基础照明到动态亮化的各类需求。

English

Outdoor waterproof LED flood lights in multiple mounting styles (wall-washing, tree-uplighting, buried, spike, tree-clamp, pole-mounted and base-mounted) for plazas, pathways, lawns and landscape projects.

High-brightness COB/SMD LED, 10W-100W customizable, 3000K-6000K and RGB optional, beam angles 15/24/36/60 degrees. Die-cast aluminum body with tempered glass, IP65-IP67, -30 to +50 Celsius, LED lifetime over 50,000 hours. Supports constant light, 0-10V, DALI, Bluetooth Mesh and DMX512 control.
